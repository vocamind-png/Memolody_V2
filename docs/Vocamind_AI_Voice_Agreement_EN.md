This Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between **Vocamind** (hereinafter referred to as the "Company") and the undersigned vocalist (hereinafter referred to as the "Talent").

**1. Scope of Work:** The Talent agrees to provide raw vocal recordings (the "Dataset") to the Company. The sole purpose of this Dataset is to be used as training material for developing, synthesizing, and creating a proprietary Artificial Intelligence voice model (the "AI Voice").

**2. Grant of Rights & Full Buyout:** The Talent irrevocably grants the Company full, exclusive, perpetual, and worldwide rights to use, process, and analyze the Dataset. The Company has the absolute right to use, distribute, and monetize any synthesized audio generated by this AI Voice across all media, present or future, without any geographical or time limitations.

**3. Compensation & Waiver of Royalties:** The Talent agrees that the initial payment provided for recording the Dataset acts as a complete and total buyout. The Talent waives all rights to any future royalties, residuals, or additional compensation arising from the use of the AI Voice or the generated audio.

**5. Non-Disclosure & Confidentiality:** The Talent agrees to keep all provided materials (such as guide tracks, scripts, and software details) strictly confidential and will not share or distribute the Dataset or any technical methodologies to third parties.

**6. Vocal Likeness & Deepfake Clauses:** The Company guarantees that the AI Voice will not be used to generate illegal, immoral content, or any material that could cause reputational damage to the Talent.
