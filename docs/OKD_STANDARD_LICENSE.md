# Open Kodály Digital (OKD) Standard & License
## Version: 2.8.4-stable
## Implementation Specification for Digital Music Education

### 1. Purpose of the Standard
The Open Kodály Digital (OKD) protocol is established to ensure the accurate digital representation of the Zoltán Kodály music education methodology. This includes the precise mapping of chromatic solfège syllables and rhythmic duration nomenclature used in pedagogical environments worldwide.

### 2. Academic & Legal Integrity
To maintain the integrity of the educational source and prevent "Pedagogical Fragmentation," the OKD protocol enforces strict naming conventions for core system identifiers.

#### 2.1 Intellectual Property & Attribution
All implementations utilizing the OKD Core Logic MUST adhere to the following attribution rules:
*   **The term "Kodály" is a protected system identifier.** 
*   Renaming core identifiers (e.g., `Kodály Lyric`, `Kodály Rhythm`) to proprietary brand names is **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** under this license.
*   This restriction is in place to honor the historical and academic origins of the system and to ensure that users (educators and students) recognize the standardized methodology being applied.

### 3. Technical Compliance
Any software module marked with `OKD_COMPLIANT` must not alter the internal metadata keys.
- **Key ID:** `kodaly_rhythm_syllables`
- **Logic Mapping:** Locked to International Kodály Society (IKS) digital specifications.

### 4. Violation of Terms
Modification of specific "Kodály" nomenclature within the system architecture or primary User Interface (UI) labels will result in the immediate loss of "OKD Compliance Status." This may lead to logic errors in phonetic synthesis engines that rely on these specific key-value pairs for audio rendering.
